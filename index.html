How the score works

Wins stolen or gifted (50%). Real wins minus the wins your scores earned against everyone. Each week you earn a fraction of a win for every team you outscored: beat 8 of 11 and that's 0.73 of a win. Score 150 and lose and you were robbed; score 85 and win and you got away with it.

Opponents got hot (20%). Each week, how many more points your opponent scored against you than they average in their other games, averaged across your games. Drawing a team on a heater is luck; drawing a team that's simply good is already covered by the first part. It overlaps with wins stolen, so it counts for less.

Injuries (30%). For every starter who missed games through injury (anyone in your lineup that week or the week before, whether drafted, picked up or traded for), each missed game counts his average points minus what a replacement-level player at his position scores in this league (the 12th best QB, 24th RB, 36th WR and 12th TE). Leaving a game early counts what he didn't score. It's measured against a league-wide replacement, not your bench, because a deep bench is skill and losing a star is luck. Players you drop stop counting.

Each part is compared across the 12 teams and combined, so 50 is average luck, 60+ is very unlucky and 40 or below has had the breaks.
